Pre-tested articles that produce good Cortex results, for use in the 2-minute demo video.

| Article | Why it works |

| en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bounded_rationality | Rich topic with a large surrounding literature |
| nature.com/articles/… (pick a Nature paper) | Exa's academic search shines here |

## Demo flow (60 seconds)

1. Open the Paul Graham essay in Chrome
2. Cortex sidebar appears; wait 2 seconds
3. Point at card 1 (Herbert Simon paper) — narrate: *"This is the paper PG is riffing on. Cortex found it in 2 seconds, without me asking."*
4. Point at card 2 (counter-argument)
5. Click card 1 — new page opens
6. Cortex sidebar updates with new sources — narrate: *"The web is now a network for me, not a haystack."*

## Recording tips

- Full-screen the browser, hide bookmarks bar
- Use Chrome incognito with only the Cortex extension enabled (clean look)
- Screen-record at 1920x1080
- Cut to the mockup image for Layer 5–7 (Cortex Pro for researchers) in the last 20 seconds
